10 Design-partner bar

  • They pick a req and see the slate without a chat blank.
  • They score a person and can reopen the why.
  • They bless a note or stage move. They hit apply. It lands in Ashby.
  • They did not re-prompt the same way they do in Claude.
If that lands, Vault, richer agents, and full ATS mode have a place. If it does not, stop adding product.
